About little Croissant

Every morning, a little croissant named Crispy sat proudly in a bakery window. While the other pastries dreamed of being sold first, Crispy dreamed of making someone smile. One rainy morning, a tired student stopped by the bakery. Seeing the golden, flaky croissant, she couldn't help but smile. She bought Crispy and enjoyed it with a warm cup of coffee while studying. That day, the student aced her exam, and she always remembered the delicious croissant that brightened a gloomy morning. And from then on, Crispy's dream came true—one smile at a time.
